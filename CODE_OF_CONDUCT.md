# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors, maintainers, and members of the Atlas Mythologica community, pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

Participation is open regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, gender expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Our goal is to build a collaborative environment focused on the preservation, documentation, and study of mythologies from around the world.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.
- Providing evidence and reliable sources when discussing mythological topics.
- Helping newcomers learn and contribute.
- Using inclusive and professional language.
- Respecting cultural traditions and historical contexts.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Hate speech.
- Spam or promotional content unrelated to the project.
- Repeatedly submitting misleading or intentionally false information.

---

# Editorial Conduct

Atlas Mythologica documents myths from many different cultures.

Contributors are expected to:

- Distinguish between primary sources, secondary sources, and personal interpretations.
- Present conflicting traditions without promoting one as the only correct version.
- Cite reliable references whenever possible.
- Avoid inserting personal beliefs into factual documentation.
- Respect the historical and cultural context of every mythology.

The purpose of the project is documentation, not theological or ideological debate.

---

# Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing contributions fairly.
- Applying project guidelines consistently.
- Maintaining a respectful environment.
- Explaining decisions whenever practical.
- Removing inappropriate content when necessary.
- Protecting the integrity of the project's data.

Maintainers may reject contributions that do not meet the project's quality or documentation standards.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub Issues
- Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Source code
- Project website
- Community spaces officially associated with Atlas Mythologica

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

---

# Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

Include, whenever possible:

- A description of the incident.
- Relevant screenshots or links.
- Dates and context.
- Any additional information that may help investigate the situation.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

---

# Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

- Warning a contributor.
- Requesting changes in behavior.
- Removing comments or content.
- Rejecting contributions.
- Temporarily suspending participation.
- Permanently banning users from community spaces.

Actions will always seek to preserve a healthy and collaborative environment.
